# 11 - Plans, Builds and Community

Where to get plans, who to watch, where to ask questions, and which books and rulebooks are worth
having on the bench. Links were checked against search indexes in November 2026; resources whose
site could not be confirmed are named in words only.

> **Heavy-driver note:** every hobby kart plan found assumes a 150-200 lb driver. The "what to beef up"
> column tells you what to change for a ~250 lb (113 kg) driver before you cut tube.

**General rule for scaling a plan to 250 lb:** go up one tube size or one wall thickness on the main rails and seat area, go up one axle size (3/4" to 1", 1" to 1-1/4"), add a third axle bearing, triangulate the seat and engine-mount areas, and lower the gearing.

## Adapting any plan for a 250 lb driver: checklist

1. **Seat and cockpit:** widen the seat bay to at least 20" between rails; most plans draw 16-18". Move the seat 2-3" rearward of the plan position and re-check rear weight bias (target 55-60% rear with driver).
2. **Main rails:** one size up (1" x 0.083" becomes 1" x 0.095" or 1-1/4" x 0.083"). Keep the plan's geometry.
3. **Seat area:** add a cross tube under the front of the seat and a diagonal from the seat back to the rear axle mounts.
4. **Rear axle:** 1" minimum with three flange bearings on a 420cc; 1-1/4" if the plan's wheelbase is over 48".
5. **Hubs:** 4-bolt, keyed and set-screwed; add a second set screw over the key.
6. **Brakes:** replace band or drum with a disc; plans older than ~2010 often still draw band brakes.
7. **Steering:** 5/8" spindles, 1/2" kingpin bolts, 3/4" steering shaft with two pillow blocks.
8. **Tires:** 4-ply rears in the largest size the plan's rear clearance allows; raise the rear rail if needed.
9. **Gearing:** add 6-10 teeth to the axle sprocket versus the plan; the torque converter will engage cleaner.
10. **Engine mount:** 3/16" plate with slots, four 3/8" Grade 8 bolts; the 420cc is roughly twice the weight of the 212cc most plans are drawn around.

## Asking for help: what to post

A forum or subreddit thread gets good answers when it includes:

- Driver weight and the intended terrain (this is the first question you will be asked otherwise).
- Engine, torque converter series and bores, axle diameter and length, hub type.
- Tube size and wall thickness, and whether it is DOM, HREW or ERW.
- A photo of the whole kart from the side and one of the problem area.
- What you already tried and what the plan or kit instructions said.

Post build logs in the DIY Go Karts forum and link to them from Reddit; the forum thread is searchable years later, the Reddit post is not.

## Quick picks

- **Free plan, scratch build, welding:** KartFab plan with the upgrades above, or the kartbuilding.net off-road kart.
- **Paid plan, off-road with suspension:** SpiderCarts Arachnid, built to the 1-1/4" tube and 1" axle changes above.
- **First read:** Finch, *Welder's Handbook*, then the IKF rulebook's tech section for what a finished kart must have.
- **First question:** DIY Go Karts forum.
